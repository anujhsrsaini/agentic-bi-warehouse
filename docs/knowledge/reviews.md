# Reviews in the Olist showcase

**Average review score** is the arithmetic mean of available 1–5 star ratings associated with delivered orders. Ask “What is the average review score?” The grain is a review, not a unique customer or an order; some orders can have multiple reviews and others none.

Ratings are not Net Promoter Score (NPS), and the results cannot establish why a score changed. See the [semantic configuration](../../config/semantic_layer.yaml) for the calculation and the demo's executed SQL for its actual sources. The data is historical.
